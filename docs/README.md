# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management process documentation. These guides provide a structured approach to planning, executing, and delivering projects with clarity, collaboration, and measurable outcomes.

## Process Overview

OctoAcme follows a lifecycle-driven approach to project management:

1. Initiation – Define the problem, stakeholders, and a high-level timeline.
2. Planning – Break work into shippable increments, estimate, and identify dependencies.
3. Execution – Build, test, review, and iterate in short cycles.
4. Release – Deploy to production, verify, and monitor.
5. Retrospective – Capture learnings and drive continuous improvement.

## Core Principles

- Customer-first: Prioritize customer value and usability.
- Iterative delivery: Deliver small, testable increments frequently.
- Clear ownership: Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead.
- Data-informed decisions: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence.
- Psychological safety: Encourage feedback, experimentation, and learning.

## Summary of OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme uses a structured, lifecycle-based project management model to align stakeholders, coordinate delivery, and maintain quality. Projects begin with initiation and stakeholder alignment, where teams define the business problem, identify stakeholders, establish success criteria, and create a lightweight plan. During planning, the work is divided into manageable and shippable increments, with dependencies, milestones, priorities, and acceptance criteria clearly documented.

Clear ownership is maintained across project managers, product managers, developers, quality assurance roles, and stakeholders. Regular communication helps teams coordinate work, identify blockers, manage dependencies, and escalate risks when necessary. Project boards, backlogs, risk registers, status updates, and other shared artifacts provide visibility into delivery progress and decisions.

Quality assurance is incorporated throughout execution and release. Teams use defined acceptance criteria, reviews, testing, release checks, and post-release validation to confirm that deliverables meet the expected standard. Risks and dependencies are tracked and communicated throughout the project lifecycle so that issues can be addressed before they significantly affect delivery.

After each release or major milestone, teams conduct retrospectives to capture lessons learned and identify improvements. Follow-up actions are assigned owners and tracked, helping OctoAcme continuously refine its processes and maintain an iterative, transparent, and repeatable approach to project delivery.
