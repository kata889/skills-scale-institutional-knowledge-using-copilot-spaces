# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy, validation planning, and release readiness. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and reduce risk before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and quality strategy for each feature or milestone
- Define and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Validate outcomes against acceptance criteria and business rules
- Identify quality risks, regressions, and release blockers
- Coordinate with Developers on testability, automation, and defect triage
- Report release readiness and quality metrics to Project Managers and stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce defects and production incidents
- Improve confidence in each release
- Maintain high-quality standards with efficient validation cycles

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and readiness reviews
- Daily standup updates on quality status and blocker tracking
- Defect triage discussions with Developers and PMs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to define test cases, coverage goals, and defect prioritization
- Partners with Project Managers to align release gates, quality risk, and milestone readiness
- Provides Product Managers with evidence of quality and user-impact risk before launch

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical direction, design guidance, and risk management for implementation decisions. They ensure the solution remains maintainable, scalable, and aligned to the intended platform strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs for major features and cross-cutting changes
- Identify technical risks, trade-offs, and mitigation paths
- Guide architectural decisions across components, integrations, and platforms
- Mentor Developers on design patterns, maintainability, and technical quality
- Ensure scalability, performance, security, and operational considerations are addressed
- Escalate high-risk technical blockers to Project Managers and leadership

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce architectural drift and technical debt
- Enable the team to make high-quality technical decisions with confidence

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture discussions
- Technical spike planning and decision logs
- Cross-team alignment on dependencies, constraints, and platform decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review implementation choices and resolve technical trade-offs
- Aligns with Product Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and impact of scope choices
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing delivery risks, dependencies, and engineering constraints

---

## Scrum Master / Iteration Lead

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Iteration Leads help the team operate effectively within the agreed delivery process. They facilitate workflows, remove blockers, and support continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Track sprint progress, velocity, and work visibility
- Identify and help remove impediments that slow delivery
- Maintain a clear project board and ensure work is ready for execution
- Coach the team on Agile practices and process consistency
- Support Project Managers with metrics, process health, and improvement actions

### Goals
- Keep the delivery team focused on agreed priorities and outcomes
- Improve predictability, flow, and team effectiveness
- Support a healthy and sustainable delivery rhythm

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Blocker escalation and process improvement sessions
- Status and process feedback for Project Managers and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Developers to keep work ready, visible, and aligned to sprint goals
- Works with Project Managers to identify blockers, process gaps, and dependency issues
- Shares team health and delivery trends with Product Managers to support prioritization and risk awareness

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and approval authority. They ensure the project remains aligned with organizational priorities and delivers measurable value.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives, success criteria, and expected outcomes
- Provide strategic direction and context for scope and prioritization trade-offs
- Review and approve major decisions related to timeline, scope, and resources
- Secure budget, staffing, and cross-functional support for delivery
- Escalate business risks and dependencies to relevant leaders
- Review project progress and validate that outcomes match business intent
- Communicate project status and decisions to broader stakeholder groups

### Goals
- Ensure project value is realized and measurable
- Maintain alignment between delivery and organizational priorities
- Remove business blockers and enable team success

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and governance checkpoints
- Monthly stakeholder updates or business reviews
- Escalation discussions for decisions affecting scope, investment, or risk

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to align product priorities with business goals and investment decisions
- Partners with Project Managers on governance, communication, and risk escalation
- Reviews progress and release readiness with delivery leads to support decision-making
- Provides approval authority when trade-offs must be made across scope, timeline, or resourcing
